What's working

  • Leads the tracked set on Visibility and Sentiment — 37 and 70 respectively, ahead of both Public Bank Berhad (31, 63) and UOB Malaysia (21, 57).
  • Clean, positive sentiment profile — 3 of 5 concept-level mentions scored positive, none negative, consistent with the aspect summary: "widely regarded as the top international bank for expats… praised for its global network, premium services, and strong digital offerings."
  • Dominant on Google AI Overview — a score of 63, the single highest result any tracked entity achieves on any engine in this dataset.
  • Consistent presence across 5 of 7 engines — GPT‑5 (47), Grok‑4 (48), Bing Copilot (43), Google AI Mode (43) and Google AI Overview (63) all surface HSBC.

Where the gap is

  • Invisible on 2 of 7 engines — DeepSeek‑R1 and Sonar Pro return a Visibility score of 0 for HSBC on both prompts. DeepSeek's answer appears to be entirely about Public Bank Berhad (77) instead.
  • Public Bank Berhad edges ahead on raw mentions (7 vs. 6) and dominates DeepSeek‑R1 (77) and ties HSBC on Bing Copilot (77 vs. 43) — a purely domestic retail bank outperforming HSBC's international positioning on two engines.
  • Thin owned-citation footprint — hsbc.com.my contributes only 3 of 120 total citations (2.5%), ranking 14th of 47 domains. The narrative is being shaped almost entirely by third-party sources.
  • The tracked set misses Malaysia's actual market leaders — the word‑cloud shows Maybank (45 mentions) and CIMB (36) dominating the "best bank" conversation, far ahead of the combined 16 mentions across all 3 tracked entities. Neither is benchmarked here (see §07).

Reading the owned-citation gap

All three tracked banks show a thin owned-citation footprint, but the pattern differs by bank. HSBC's 3 citations to hsbc.com.my are the strongest of the three, yet still only half of one percent of the way to matching its mention share. UOB pulls 2 citations to uob.com.my. Public Bank Berhad — despite leading all three on raw mentions (7) and dominating two engines outright — has no owned-domain citations at all in this dataset; every AI answer that surfaces it is relying entirely on third-party sources.

For a category this thin on owned pull-through across the board, the opportunity is less about defending share from competitors and more about giving AI engines any citable, authoritative HSBC content to draw on for these prompts (see §07 and §08).

07

Competitive gaps & opportunities

Ranked by how much they're likely to move HSBC's standing on these prompts.

High

HSBC is invisible on 2 of 7 engines

DeepSeek‑R1 and Sonar Pro return a Visibility score of 0 for HSBC on both prompts. DeepSeek's answer instead appears built almost entirely around Public Bank Berhad (77), while Sonar Pro doesn't surface any of the 3 tracked banks at all — likely defaulting to Malaysia's larger local banks instead.

Recommended actionInvestigate what content these two engines draw on for Malaysia banking queries and whether HSBC has any indexable presence in it — this is the single biggest lever on the overall Visibility score.
High

The tracked benchmark set misses Malaysia's market leaders

The word‑cloud shows Maybank (45 mentions) and CIMB (36) dominating the "best bank in Malaysia" conversation — more than double the combined 16 mentions across all 3 tracked entities. Standard Chartered (14), Hong Leong Bank (11) and RHB (8) also outrank UOB Malaysia (3) as competitors in the international/large-bank conversation.

Recommended actionExpand the tracked entity set to include Maybank, CIMB, Standard Chartered and Hong Leong Bank for a competitive picture that reflects what AI engines are actually comparing HSBC against.
Medium

A domestic retail bank is out-mentioning HSBC

Public Bank Berhad — a purely domestic retail bank with no international positioning — edges HSBC on raw mentions (7 vs. 6) and dominates two engines outright, despite having zero owned-domain citations anywhere in this dataset. Its visibility is being carried entirely by third parties.

Recommended actionWorth understanding qualitatively why DeepSeek and Bing default to Public Bank Berhad — it may simply reflect Malaysia's largest branch network and retail footprint rather than anything HSBC can directly counter.
Medium

Content-marketing competitors own the "best bank" search intent

Four remittance/fintech blogs (Wise, WorldFirst, Ace Money Transfer, Airwallex) contribute 18 combined citations with comparison-style "best bank" content — a content category HSBC has no visible presence in. Meanwhile three independent expat guides carry 9 citations answering almost exactly HSBC's "best international bank" positioning, without HSBC's own voice in the mix.

Recommended actionPublish HSBC-authored comparison/guide content targeting "best bank in Malaysia for expats" style queries — the fintech blogs prove the format works; HSBC currently isn't competing in it directly.
Low

Sample size is small — treat findings as directional

This run covers 2 prompts and a single one-day snapshot, with only 16 combined mentions across the 3 tracked entities. Individual engine scores (especially the 0s on DeepSeek and Sonar Pro) could shift meaningfully with a larger prompt set or a different capture date.

Recommended actionNo urgent action needed, but avoid treating any single number here — especially the engine-level zeros — as a fixed, permanent state without a follow-up run.
